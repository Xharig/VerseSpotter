A trainer in your browser that works like a driving-theory app: you see an image, pick one of three ships and get your results at the end. What you got wrong comes back soon, what you know comes up less often.

## What VerseSpotter does

- **Six image types from easy to hard:** ship renders to warm up, outline, distance, silhouette in flight, everything mixed and HUD hologram
- **Spaced repetition:** every ship has five boxes per image type; misses come back soon, known ships less often
- **Difficulty that grows with you:** the better you know a ship, the more similar the wrong answers get
- **10, 20, 30 or 40 questions**, optionally in **combat mode** with 5 seconds per image
- **Results with a rank** (Ace, Spotter, Cadet, Recruit), a list of misses with the correct ship and a **result to copy** for Discord or forums
- **Ship catalogue** with every flight-ready spaceship to look up
- **Works with mouse or keyboard** (1, 2, 3 and Enter), on phones and at 200 % zoom

<summary><b>Contributing images</b></summary>

The HUD and silhouette images come from the community. Every image credits its collector, and all of them are listed on the **Thanks & licences** page. Found a wrong image? Every image has a **"Report image"** button.

No cookies, no IP addresses, no account. Your progress stays in your own browser. Counting happens without any identifier, only "how many". Details on the [Thanks & licences](https://versespotter.xharig.com/danke.html#datenschutz) page.
