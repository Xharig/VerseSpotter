# Changelog

[Deutsch](CHANGELOG.md) · **English**

## v1.0.0 - 2026-10-02

The first launch: learn to recognise ships like in a driving-theory app. Look at the image, pick one of three ships, get your results — and whatever you got wrong comes back soon.

### New
- Training with four image types: HUD hologram, silhouette, mixed and ship renders to warm up
- Spaced repetition with five boxes per ship and image type; wrong answers get more similar as you improve
- 10, 20, 30 or 40 questions, combat mode with 5 seconds per image
- Results with a rank, a list of misses and a result to copy for Discord
- Ship catalogue with 180 flight-ready spaceships, search and size filter
- "Report image" on every in-game image
- German and English, works with mouse and keyboard
